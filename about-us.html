Magical Hands physiotherapy clinic was the brain child of its founders Mr. Kowcigan Jambulingam and Mr. Pugalendran Rajendran. They are truly passionate about providing quality rehabilitative services at an affordable cost to everyone.

When magical hands was initially started and it was solely providing physiotherapy services. But to satisfy the growing demands of the customers, it has now incorporated other holistic medicinal approaches such as yoga therapy, pilates, Reiki healing, sports and remedial massage therapy, meditation

KJ graduated from the Tamil Nadu Dr M.G. R University, India in 2009 with the Bachelors degree in Physiotherapy. KJ’s specialist interest is in manual therapy treating predominantly musculo-skeletal and neurological conditions. His keen affinity towards manual therapy has lead him to learn techniques like muligan’s mobilisation, myofascial release, taping etc., and keep him up to date with all latest developments in the world of physiotherapy.

KJ has gained extensive clinical knowledge working alongside renowned sports physicians, cardiologists, neurologists, orthopaedic surgeons in India. Apart from his private practice he is also working in the NHS as a senior community physiotherapist. He has also completed his masters of Business Administration in Healthcare Management. This helps him in establishing his private practice with efficiency.

Pugal is a Qualified Physiotherapist, who has complete his physiotherapy Bachelors degree in India 2009. He has completed his education with gold medal & awarded as PHYSIO TOPPER in 2009.He has worked in neurological departments as a neuro vestibular physiotherapist with a renowned neurologist Dr. Radha Krishnan MD.,DM (Neurologist). Together they have conducted research on patients suffering from vertigo and Neuro vestibular disorders. Eventually he has mastered in neuro vestibular rehabilitation techniques and has successfully treated number of patients with his treatment technique.

Under his belt, he has exquisite record of experience working in the UK as a physiotherapist in the private & NHS settings. Through his experience, he has gained immense knowledge & expertise that he uses to treat his patients and create success stories with almost all the cases.
