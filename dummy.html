When magical hands was initially started and it was solely providing physiotherapy services. KJ graduated from the Tamil Nadu Dr M.G. R University, India in 2009 with the Bachelors degree in Physiotherapy. KJ’s specialist interest is in manual therapy treating predominantly musculo-skeletal and neurological conditions. His keen affinity towards manual therapy has lead him to learn techniques like muligan’s mobilisation, myofascial release, taping etc., and keep him up to date with all latest developments in the world of physiotherapy.

KJ has gained extensive clinical knowledge working alongside renowned sports physicians, cardiologists, neurologists, orthopaedic surgeons in India. Apart from his private practice he is also working in the NHS as a senior community physiotherapist. He has also completed his masters of Business Administration in Healthcare Management. This helps him in establishing his private practice with efficiency.

Pugal is a Qualified Physiotherapist, who has complete his physiotherapy Bachelors degree in India 2009. Eventually he has mastered in neuro vestibular rehabilitation techniques and has successfully treated number of patients with his treatment technique.

Under his belt, he has exquisite record of experience working in the UK as a physiotherapist in the private & NHS settings. Through his experience, he has gained immense knowledge & expertise that he uses to treat his patients and create success stories with almost all the cases.
